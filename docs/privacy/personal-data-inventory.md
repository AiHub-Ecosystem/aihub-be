# AIHUB personal data inventory

**Record owner:** AIHUB engineering<br>
**Last checked:** 2026-10-13<br>
**Scope:** Production and Sandbox behavior represented by this repository and its deployment documentation.

This is an engineering inventory, not a legal classification or legal advice.
The classifications below describe data operationally, using the vocabulary in
the [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md). A stated expiry is not evidence that a row or
object is physically deleted: enforcement gaps are called out separately.
Deployment facts that are not established by repository evidence are marked
unknown. A pseudonymous identifier can still identify someone when linked to
other data. The shared production host and its operators have root-equivalent
access to AIHUB data hosted there; the maintainer has accepted the current
absence of at-rest encryption on that VPS, with the reason and scope recorded
below.

### Writing Task 1 chart uploads

The upload record stores the Organization, End User, API-key environment,
declared content type and byte size, object keys, and lifecycle timestamps.
At completion, AIHUB decodes and re-encodes the image, normalizes its
orientation, and removes embedded source metadata before publication. The
chart content itself may still contain personal information.
Staging objects stay private and are eligible for deletion 24 hours after the
one-hour intent expires. Published images are publicly readable by anyone who
has the URL; their deletion deadline is 24 hours after publication, handled by
the 15-minute sweep at the first successful run after expiry. SeaweedFS bucket
backups and cache/CDN deletion propagation have not been established.

## Backup copies

- A manual pg_dump -Fc is stored under ~/aihub-backups on the same VPS before
  releases or usage pruning. Issue #281 notes that aihub_sandbox is not included
  in any routine backup. There is no scheduled backup, off-host copy, or
  documented backup retention period yet. Deleting a live row does not remove
  a dump already made. The off-host destination, retention, restore schedule,
  and key holder remain open under [#281](https://github.com/AiHub-Ecosystem/aihub-be/issues/281).
- Avatar, Speaking audio, and Writing chart object backups are not yet established; the
  destination, retention, encryption, and restore consistency are open under
  [#386](https://github.com/AiHub-Ecosystem/aihub-be/issues/386).
- The accepted risk for unencrypted live VPS data does not decide backup
  encryption. Issue #281 requires a Postgres dump to be encrypted before it
  leaves the host; the mechanism, key holder, destination, and retention are
  still unknown. Object-backup requirements remain under #386.

The Customer Web BFF is a first-party integration rather than a third-party
processor: it receives short-lived User Access JWTs and membership/grading
responses. It can cache a membership decision for up to five minutes in the
sandbox design. Its hosting country, logs, and cache deletion behavior are
outside this repository's evidence; the integration boundary is described in
[ADR-0081](../adr/0081-aihub-owned-customer-web-web-sessions.md). Its separate
hosting, logs, and cache deletion facts remain unknown; [#380](https://github.com/AiHub-Ecosystem/aihub-be/issues/380)
owns the unresolved recipient/contract disclosure, and the Customer Web owner
must provide deployment evidence before these facts can be marked verified.

That does not constrain root-equivalent host operators, the shared Postgres

## Residency, consent, and legal questions

- AIHUB does not persist a consent record for these flows. Whether consent is
  required, who must obtain it (including for minors), and how withdrawal
  changes processing are questions for counsel and customer agreements; see
  [#380](https://github.com/AiHub-Ecosystem/aihub-be/issues/380).
- The current VPS, Redis, Resend, monitoring receiver, AI services/model
  providers, Vault, and any future backup destination do not have a verified
  physical country in this record. The SeaweedFS S3 region setting is not
  physical-location evidence.
- Counsel under #380 must determine AIHUB's role for each category, applicable
  law and legal basis, rights/response timelines, minors' requirements,
  cross-border transfer duties, customer DPA/subprocessor disclosures and
  change notices, and breach-notification duties and deadlines. This record
  makes no legal conclusion.

## Change review

For any migration that adds or changes a column holding personal data or an
identifier linkable to a person, update this record with its category, purpose,
classification, stores/recipients, retention trigger, deletion behavior,
residency evidence, and at-rest status. The [pull request template](../../.github/pull_request_template.md)
requires this review.
