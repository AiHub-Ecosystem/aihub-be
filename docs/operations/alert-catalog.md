# AIHUB host and dependency alerts

This is the host/dependency slice contributed by [#474](https://github.com/AiHub-Ecosystem/aihub-be/issues/474) to the broader alert catalog tracked by [#412](https://github.com/AiHub-Ecosystem/aihub-be/issues/412). It does not claim that #412's application, delivery, watchdog, or quarterly-review criteria are complete.

The configured owner label is `aihub-operations`. Routes below were observed in the existing Alertmanager: `warning` goes to `discord-warning`; `critical` goes to `discord-critical`, Pushover, and Telegram. The route is current runtime configuration, not a substitute for #412's severity/notification policy decision. Runbook links point to [AIHUB observability](aihub-observability.md).

The responder for every alert in this catalog is the repository maintainer, @lengocanh2005it (#291), until #293 sets an on-call rotation.

Disk 80%, PostgreSQL 80% of `max_connections`, and certificate 14 days are defined by #474 and its linked decisions. The five-second clock threshold is a conservative proposal: one-sixth of the 30-second TOTP step and one-twelfth of the 60-second JWT/assertion skew allowance. Other non-issue-defined thresholds/severity assignments are configured proposals pending #412 review. cAdvisor cannot report stopped-container history. The Redis memory threshold must be updated if the observed `maxmemory` changes. Delivery of the three #291 alerts was exercised on 2026-10-19 through both routes and confirmed received by the responder; the #474 rules have not been deliberately fired. Do not treat this scoped list as completion of #412.
