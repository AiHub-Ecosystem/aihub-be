# Organization membership mutation boundary

Status: accepted

Organization membership removal means disabling the durable membership, never physical deletion. Management targets use the immutable public username. Role changes use `PATCH /v1/organizations/:organizationId/members/:username` with a role body, while disabling uses `DELETE` on the same resource; both return the resulting membership in the shared success envelope. The ordinary role patch cannot assign `owner`; `POST /v1/organizations/:organizationId/members/:username/transfer` is the only owner-promotion path and atomically makes the target an owner and the initiating owner an admin. Owners may change or disable any target subject to the zero-owner invariant, while admins may change or disable only targets whose current role is `member` (including promotion to `admin`). Members may disable themselves; owners may self-demote or self-disable only when another owner remains, and self-promotion is forbidden. A two-request sequence is not a transfer substitute. Disabled memberships are not reactivated by these endpoints and are restored only through invitation acceptance. Role/disable operations are state-idempotent and serialize organization and relevant membership rows in one transaction; public failures are `403` for policy denial, `404` for unknown or cross-organization targets, and `409` for zero-owner conflicts. Disable takes effect on the next Bearer membership/management authorization check and does not change the separate `X-API-Key`, User Assertion, or downstream grading boundaries. No separate idempotency record or database trigger is added because the durable state transition and owner invariant are the source of truth.
